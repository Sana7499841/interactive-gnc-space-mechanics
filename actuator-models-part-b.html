From Actuator Capability to Control Allocation

Part B determines what the spacecraft actuator system can physically achieve and what limits that capability.

The next major problem is

\[ \boxed{ \text{Given a desired spacecraft wrench, which actuators should be commanded?} }. \]

The transition is

\[ \boxed{\text{Desired Wrench}} \rightarrow \boxed{\text{Control Allocation}} \rightarrow \boxed{\text{Individual Actuator Commands}} \rightarrow \boxed{\text{Realized Wrench}}. \]

The detailed allocation algorithms belong in the later section on Control Allocation and Actuator Commanding.
