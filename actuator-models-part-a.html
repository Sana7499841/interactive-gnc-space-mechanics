From Individual Actuator Physics to Complete Control Authority

The next question is no longer

\[ \boxed{ \text{How does one actuator work?} } \]

but

\[ \boxed{ \text{What can the complete installed actuator system actually achieve?} }. \]

Part B therefore develops actuator authority at the spacecraft-system level, including effectiveness-matrix rank, singular values, redundancy, reachable wrench sets, constraints, uncertainty, failures, and verification.
