4.4 Part A — Actuator Fundamentals, Physics, and Models

Part A develops the physical and mathematical foundations required to understand how spacecraft actuators generate force, torque, impulse, and momentum exchange.

The emphasis is on understanding the actuator itself: its operating principle, geometry, physical output, dynamics, and its interaction with spacecraft motion.

The progression is

\[ \boxed{\text{Actuator Fundamentals}} \rightarrow \boxed{\text{Reaction Wheels}} \rightarrow \boxed{\text{CMGs}} \rightarrow \boxed{\text{Magnetorquers}} \rightarrow \boxed{\text{Thrusters}} \rightarrow \boxed{\text{Other Actuators}}. \]
